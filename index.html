I'm Emma, a policy and strategic communications professional and journalist with experience across government, digital transformation, technology and public services. I work where journalism, editorial, policy and emerging technology meet, helping complex ideas reach the people who need to understand and act on them.

As Head of Content at Public Digital, a consultancy founded by the leaders of the UK Government Digital Service, I worked alongside consultants advising governments and public institutions around the world. I helped develop and publish their policy thinking, including parliamentary submissions on government customer service and policy memoranda for House of Commons Select Committees, as well as editorial content, social and video. That work gave me a precise understanding of the gap between policy intent and the experience of the people it is meant to serve, and of what it takes for digital change and AI to close it.

I've written for The Sydney Morning Herald, The Age, Forbes, Fast Company, MSN and The Independent, covering consumer issues, policy, work and technology.

Previously, I built the content and social function at Threecolts, a $90 million technology startup. I led funding communications, developed executive thought leadership for US publications, and helped make a complex software business understandable to customers and the market.

Before that, I worked in film and theatre with BAFTA- and Oscar-nominated teams. That's where I learned to find the human story inside complex material.

Across policy, journalism and communications, my approach is consistent: get close to the people doing the work, ask the questions that matter, and find the evidence that makes an argument worth believing.
